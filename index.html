OUR MINISTRY

Living in God's Presence

At God's Presence Ministries, our desire is to see all life lived in God's presence, where His presence finds its fullest expression in humanity.

Our Mission

HOW WE LIVE OUR VISION

Our Approach to Life

Growing together in faith, unity, and fellowship with one another.

Life Application

Applying God's Word to our everyday choices and actions.
